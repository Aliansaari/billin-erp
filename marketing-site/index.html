Scan. Quantity. Enter. That's it. Manas is built for shops where the next person in line shouldn't have to wait. Every keystroke matters — and we counted them.

  • Camera barcode scanning on iOS & Android, USB scanners on desktop
  • Auto-fill from product master: HSN, GST, last rate, supplier
  • Keyboard-first: ⌘K global search, F6 new bill, F7 print preview

02 / sync

Real-time LAN sync. No cloud needed.

Your database lives on your machine. Ten computers in your shop see the same numbers, instantly. Works without internet. Pays nothing every month. You own your data.

  • PostgreSQL backend you can self-host — or run on the manager's PC
  • 10 concurrent users with role-based access (Admin, Cashier, Inventory, Accountant)
  • Auto-backup every hour, daily, weekly — restorable in one click
  • Audit trail on every change: who, when, what was old, what's new
03 / compliance

GST done. Quietly. Correctly.

GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B come out as ready-to-upload exports. HSN summaries, CGST / SGST / IGST per line, audit-trailed round-off. The math is invisible. The compliance is exact.

  • Per-line HSN, CGST, SGST, IGST and Cess — computed live
  • Product-wise or transaction-wise GST mode (your call)
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B export in one click, JSON or Excel
  • Full audit trail — every edit logged for tax assessments

Software that respects the eight hours you'll spend in it.

Most billing software is built like it was paid for by the screenshot. Manas isn't. We obsessed over the kerning, the cursor, the milliseconds. The result is a tool that gets out of your way — and stays out.

Warm cream palette. Monospaced numbers that line up. Skeleton placeholders that don't flash. The kind of details you only notice after you've used it for a week.
