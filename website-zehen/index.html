03 WhatsApp bot

Run the shop
from WhatsApp.

ZEHEN talks to your customers and to you — on the app everyone already has open. No new software to teach, no portal to log into.

  • Auto-send everything. The moment a bill is saved, the invoice PDF reaches the customer. Receipts and monthly statements go out on their own.
  • Your private command centre. Text dues, sales, low stock or cash and the owner panel replies instantly — top dues, today's sales, reorder list, bank balances.
  • Customers serve themselves. A buyer messages your number for their balance, past bills or a statement PDF — reply with a bill number, get the PDF back.
  • Every reply is switchable. Turn each capability on or off per number. Official Cloud API or your own WhatsApp — your choice.
