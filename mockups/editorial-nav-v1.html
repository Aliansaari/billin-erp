04

Active-pill: today vs proposed

With 11 pills on the bar, today's solid-accent fill on the active item shouts. The proposed treatment uses four quiet signals stacked — subtle bg, bolder label, filled-style icon, and a 2px accent indicator — for a more enterprise feel that doesn't fight the rest of the chrome.

Side by side same content · two treatments
Today · solid accent

Solid accent fill demands the eye. On a bar of 11 pills it reads as a billboard — fine on a sidebar, loud on a horizontal bar.

Proposed · 4-signal stack

5% white tint + 600 weight label + accent-tinted icon + 2px bottom indicator. Information louder, chrome quieter. Linear / Stripe / Vercel all do variants of this.

06

Three personas, one design

The chrome adapts to who's using it. SMB sees only the command bar. Mid-market adds the workspace card. Enterprise unlocks the status strip plus multi-tab. Auto-detected from companies.length, auditEnabled, multiBranch; user can override in Settings.

Command bar only. Workspace card hidden — there's no other company to switch to. Settings shows the toggle if they later add one.

Workspace card appears with company name. Click opens the switcher with companies, branches, and FY in one panel. Bell shows unread.

08

Three calls before I build

1 · Status strip + tabs
Ship now (gated, off by default) or stub for v2? I lean ship-now — building them later means re-touching every file.
2 · Inline counters
Wire to a new /nav/counters aggregate endpoint, or render visually-empty for now and hook the data when ready? I lean wire-now — it's the single best "alive" signal on the page.
3 · Active-pill style
Keep today's solid accent fill, or switch to the proposed 4-signal stack? Section 04 has both side by side — your call.
