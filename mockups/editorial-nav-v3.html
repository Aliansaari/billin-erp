A · Why grouping
7 verbs, not 11.
Sales/Purchase/Inventory/Banking each subsume their lists, returns, and party screens. Reports stays a single pill. Tally Alt+S still jumps straight to "New Sale" — no extra clicks for veterans.
B · Right cluster
Three, not five.
Search · Bell · Avatar. Theme and Settings move into the avatar dropdown — they're configured rarely, don't deserve permanent chrome. Saves 76 px on every render.
C · The same dropdowns
Tally pop-up, reused.
Click "Sales ▾" → opens the same MenuPopup that Alt+S opens. One mental model, two ways in. Letters on the right of each row are direct shortcuts.
